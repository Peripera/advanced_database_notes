## What I understood
- I understood that a transaction is a group of SQL changes that should work as one unit. For example, when money moves from one account to another, the debit and the credit must both happen. If only one part happens, the data becomes wrong. COMMIT saves the changes, ROLLBACK cancels them, and SAVEPOINT lets me undo only part of the work.
